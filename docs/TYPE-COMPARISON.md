## Font families

- **iOS:** Open Sans, PT Serif, Spectral. Standard template: Spectral for headlines and article body, Open Sans for UI and labels, PT Serif for some cell headlines.
- **Android:** Open Sans, Open Sans Condensed, PT Serif, Playfair Display, Roboto, Spectral. PT Serif for headlines and article body in most markets, Open Sans for UI, Roboto (system) for settings and paywall text, Playfair Display for the paywall heading.
- **Biggest difference:** the serif. iOS sets headlines and article text in Spectral; Android sets them in PT Serif. Both apps bundle both fonts.
- **Legacy template (iOS, BS, DP, HC, MC, OS, SS, VP):** 7 extra styles plus different values for many others (see each note).
