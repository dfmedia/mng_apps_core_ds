# Icon replacements (DEC-29)

Generated by `scripts/build_icon_replacements.py`. Do not edit by hand.

Every shipped UI icon gets a professionally drawn vector: the closest **Material Symbol** (Android and Core; Apache-2.0, so the SVGs live in the repos) and the closest **SF Symbol** (iOS; exported on Karl's Mac by `scripts/sf_symbols_vectors.swift`, kept out of the repos while they are public). Shipped PNGs are not traced (DEC-29). What ships today stays documented in docs/ICON-NAMES.md and docs/ICON-SOURCES.md (rule 1).

**52 icon names:** 43 stock symbol, 2 stock symbol for some files, redraw for others, 5 redraw, 1 open: karl to decide, 1 asked of the apps team. Material SVGs: 30 in `icons/material/` (Core), 2 in `mng_apps_android_ds/icons/material/` (Android only). SF Symbols needed: 54.

Brand marks (`brand-*`) are not here: they stay the brands' own artwork (DEC-26).
