## Header band (iOS) and status bar (Android): market overrides

DEC-15 (Karl, 2026-11-05): `header` and `statusbar` follow the top bar (`navbar`). In these markets the app ships them different from its own top bar, mostly because the top bar is white, so the majority build fails contrast. **Karl accepted every suggested override on 2026-11-05** (data/shell-overrides.json): these markets get an override token with the shipped color (`color.ios.<m>.header` / `color.android.<m>.statusbar`), built in tokens, Swift, Kotlin and Figma. Drawn majority vs override in Figma (iOS file: Components · App shell › 01 · Header band exceptions; Android file: › 01 · Status bar exceptions, with an Android 14 and below vs 15+ comparison). Android: the override is what Android 14 and below shows (white icons); on Android 15+ the status bar is navbar, with dark icons only where the shipped bar is exactly #FFFFFF (EdgeToEdgeUtil.java:30; Ameer M., confirmed 2026-11-10; tokens `statusbar.android.<m>.icons` / `icons15`).

Foreground: iOS header text is `Style.json cell-tertiary-headertext` (#ffffff in all 77 markets). Android status-bar icons are light by default; the main theme never sets `windowLightStatusBar` (MISSING-ASSETS 5.6). Apps team (Ameer M., 2026-11-05 from code; confirmed on device 2026-11-10): on Android 14 and below the icons are white, so the dark overrides are needed; on Android 15+ the status bar is navbar and the app picks dark icons only when that color is exactly #FFFFFF (EdgeToEdgeUtil.java:30); every other color keeps white icons (confirmed 2026-11-10, Android 16 emulator, Denver Post). So DP, MC, NYDN, OS, SS and VP get dark icons; TSAR (#F5F5F5) keeps white icons on a near-white bar (IMPROVEMENTS CLR-09). Both versions are built (DEC-15): `statusbar` + `icons` (14 and below), navbar + `icons15` (15+). Dark and light icon colors are drawn by the system; the contrast above uses black and white.
