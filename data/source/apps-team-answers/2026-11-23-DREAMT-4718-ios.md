**1.12 (iOS)** Yes, it never appears on iOS, so it's safe to drop.

- iOS has no Amazon icon at all. The only Amazon UI is a text-only "Log in with Amazon" button (orange `#FF9900`, black text) in TroncKit's native login screen.
- That screen only opens when Auth0 is off, and Auth0 is on in all 78 markets. The paywall, onboarding and Your News all go to the Auth0 page instead.
- No market's config turns on an Amazon login provider either.
