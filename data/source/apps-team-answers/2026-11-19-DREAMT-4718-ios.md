**5.10** Meter paywall purchase button:

1. **Uppercase:** always. Every market sets the text to `"Paywall_SubscriptionOffers_CallToAction".localized.uppercased()`, which is "Try one month free" in all 78. So it's forced uppercase, and the text is the same everywhere.
2. **Height:** the constraint is "at least 45 pt", but the label is one line in a fixed 14 pt font, so in practice it's always 45.
3. **Legacy markets:** no difference. All 78 use the same storyboard button: radius 8, a 0.7 pt border in the fill color, Open Sans Bold 14. Only the fill color varies by market, and it always equals `paywallPrimaryActionColor`.
4. **Label color:** white in every market.
