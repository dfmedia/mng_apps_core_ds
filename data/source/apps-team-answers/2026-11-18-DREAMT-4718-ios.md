iOS answers for DREAMT-4718 and MISSING-ASSETS.md. All values are from code at `f79fe3339`. They're also in the style guide (v21): https://claude.ai/artifact/V364juyHFU3STfVZ6EgjMc

**Read these first**

- **Login is the Auth0 web view in every market.** The native Log In / Register buttons and the social sign-in row (Facebook / Google / Amazon) are never shown, so their values in the guide are marked "not shown in production".
- **"Manage App Subscription" is white text on a white button in 7 markets:** DP, MC, NYDN, OS, SS, TSAR and VP. The button is filled with the nav bar color, and those markets have a white nav bar. This comes from the code and hasn't been checked on a device.
- **iOS has no adhesion (sticky) banner.**

**5.4 Buttons**

- **Primary button height:** iOS has no single primary button. The ones that ship:
  - Meter paywall purchase button: at least 45 pt tall (45 measured on TDP), radius 8, Open Sans Bold 14, filled with `paywallPrimaryActionColor`
  - Manage App Subscription: 44 pt, radius 8
  - Push prompt Yes: 40 pt
  - Newsletters login: at least 52 pt
  - A 44 pt guideline fits these.
- **Paywall Log In link:**
  - It's one centered button below the purchase button. The text is set per market: the default is "Have an account? / Log In" with only "Log In" underlined, and TDP shows "Already a Subscriber? Log in to your Denver post account". The purchase button text varies the same way (default "Subscription Offers", TDP "TRY ONE MONTH FREE").
  - Font: Open Sans Regular 14. BS, DP, HC, MC, OS and SS use 11, and NYDN and VP use 12.
- **Pressed state:** none of these buttons has a custom one. The title fades and the fill stays the same.

**5.5 App shell sizes**

- **Nav bar:** 44 pt, logo at its natural size.
- **Section bar:**
  - 44 pt tall, 15 pt between tabs.
  - Selected tab: 4 pt underline that fades in over 0.32 s.
- **Breaking-news banner:** 35 pt, Open Sans Bold 14.
- **Section header band:** 30 pt, 10 pt text inset.
- **Tab bar:**
  - iOS default height and system font.
  - On iOS 26: system 9 pt medium titles, and "TOP NEWS" / "YOUR NEWS" become "NEWS" / "YOU".

**5.6 Other controls**

- **Push prompt:**
  - Both buttons are 40 pt tall with radius 5.
  - Yes: Open Sans Bold 14.
  - No: Open Sans Regular 14, underlined in every market except VP, with no fill.
- **Manage subscription and Delete account:** these are Settings rows, not buttons. Delete account is system red.
  - The Manage subscription modal has two 44 pt buttons with radius 8: one filled with the nav bar color, and a white Cancel with a 1 pt black border.
- **Savings pill:**
  - Pill: 110 × 30, white fill, a 2 pt border in `subscriptionTintColor`, radius 13. It's inset 10 pt from the edge on phone and 35 pt on iPad.
  - Text: Open Sans Bold 13 in the same color.
- **Social sign-in:**
  - Only on the Newsletters login, with Apple and Google.
  - 52 pt tall, radius 9, Open Sans SemiBold 18 scaled with Dynamic Type.
  - 1 pt outline, 22 pt icon with a 12 pt gap.
- **Segmented control (For You / Saved / Alerts):**
  - iOS system style, the same in every market: a white selected segment on #F2F2F7 and the system font.
  - It sits in a 50 pt band.
- **Loading indicator:** a large system spinner on a white card with radius 12.
  - The spinner is #004E87 in 66 markets, including MNG markets, and #005F7E in DP and VP.
  - Other colors: BS #DAA520, HC #4FA8D1, TDCD #5B9BC8 and TIVDB #E0E2E4.

**5.7 Checkbox:** the 17 × 18 images are centered at natural size in a 30 × 30 button. `Uncheck-Grey` isn't used.

**5.8 Ads:**

- First article slot: 320×50 on phone, 728×90 on tablet.
- All other slots: 300×250.
- These are the same in every market and match Android.
- When the view is too narrow for the ad, it falls back to 320×50. There's no adhesion banner.

**4.1** NYDN bundles Open Sans Condensed Bold, Light and Light Italic.

**4.2** Article text has 6 steps: ×0.85, ×1.0 (default), ×1.15, ×1.30, ×1.45 and ×1.60. If the reader follows the iOS text size instead, sizes scale with it.

**4.3** Open Sans weights:

- **Light:** used in the subscription option description (12), section search results (20) and message / empty-state views.
- **ExtraBold:** only used to measure tab widths. It's never drawn.
- **Light Italic and ExtraBold Italic:** not used.

**4.4 Missing text styles**

- **Section tabs:** Open Sans Bold 11. These markets use SemiBold 12: BS, CT, DP, HC, MC, NYDN, OS, SS, TDP, TDPS, VP and TIVDB.
- **Settings rows:** Open Sans Regular 14. BS, HC and SS use 11, and NYDN uses 12.
- **Settings headers:** Open Sans Bold 14, uppercase, in a 30 pt band. NYDN uses Open Sans Condensed Bold 14.
- **Search results:**
  - Story results use `cell-headline` at the xx-small size.
  - Section results use Open Sans Light 20.
- Style.json `nav-title`, `tab-item` and `blocker-login` aren't read by any iOS code.

**5.1 Shadows:** there's no shared elevation scale. Each shadow is set where it's used, and all are black:

| Where | Opacity | Radius | Offset |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top banner alert | 0.18 | 14 | 0, 8 |
| Persistent audio player | 0.20 | 10 | 0, −2 |
| Web view sheet | 0.15 | 10 | 0, 4 |
| Alert with image | 0.5 | 5 | 2, 2 |
| Mini media player | 0.3 | 4 | 0, 1 |
| Newsletters back button | 0.15 | 3 | 0, 1 |
| Photo push transition | 0.4 | 6 | 0, −3 |

Recirculation cards take their shadow from the article style. They're shadow tokens in the guide, converted to CSS.

**5.2 Animations:** there's no shared spec. Durations are set where they're used:

- **0.3 s:** most fades and expands.
- **0.5 s:** recirculation, games landing and live blog. Section front and Your News use 0.5 s after a 0.2 s delay, ease-out.
- **0.32 s:** section tab underline.
- **Others:** 0.18 s article layout, 0.1 s section front top region, 0.72 s tournament loading, 1.5 s widget shimmer.
- The full table is in the guide under Layout → Motion.

**Icons and logos**

- **1.1:** no vector masters exist in the repo. 213 of the 218 images are PNG exports, and the only vectors are the 4 Author Profile SVGs.
- **1.2:** `ManageSubscriptionIcon` is the 100 pt logo on the Manage Subscription modal. It has no image file, so that area is blank.
- **1.3:** the Saved tab still calls `UIImage(systemName: "saved")`, which returns no image.
- **1.4:**
  - **Home tab:** 9 markets have their own Home icon: CT, BS, DP, MC, NYDN, SS, VP, HC and OS. Every other market uses Font Awesome `home`.
  - **For You tab:** the same 9 markets have their own selected icon.
  - **Sections and Alerts:** Font Awesome `bars` and `bell` in every market. No market overrides them.
  - **Files:** all PNG, no vectors.
- **1.5:** these icons have no separate design:
  - ±15 s skip uses SF Symbols `gobackward.15` / `goforward.15`.
  - Text size, Games, Filter and Follow / Unfollow are PNGs.
  - iOS can switch any of them to SF Symbols.
- **1.6:** that works on iOS. Each set is one glyph saved as separate PNGs, so one template image tinted per market can replace it.
- **1.7:** no. The For You icon exists only as the `YourNews-*` PNGs.
- **1.8:** there's no Digital tab anymore. It was an alternate name for the eNewspaper tab, and that code is commented out.
- **1.9:** the tournament loading symbols are `soccerball.inverse`, `american.football.fill`, `baseball.fill`, `basketball.fill` and `hockey.puck.fill`, with `sportscourt.fill` as the fallback. All are 72 pt.
- **1.11:** the repo has no official brand vectors. Every brand mark is PNG except the Author Profile SVGs (Bluesky, Facebook, Instagram, X), so current vectors would come from each brand's press kit.
- **2.1:** 31 markets ship their main logo and lettermark as vectors, and PAC, SIT and TPD have vector logos too.
- **2.2:** markets without their own onboarding art show the shared `mng-top-image-view` image.
